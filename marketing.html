Every number, back to its filing.

Type a ticker. See what the company does, whether it is profitable, whether it is growing, and how it is priced against its own history — on one screen. No account, no subscription, no telemetry. Everything works offline once it is downloaded.

The rule the whole app rests on: the model never produces a number. Every figure is computed from SEC filings in code. The model is handed those figures and asked only for words — and what it writes is checked against them before you see it.

What it does

Snapshot

One screen per company: what it does, profitability, growth, and price against its own multi-year median. Every cell taps through to its explanation and its filing.

Twelve years, charted

Revenue, three margins, net income, free cash flow, share count, net debt, dividends. The exact table is one tap away, the CSV one more.

Screens

Named, saved, re-run on demand. Describe one in plain English and the app builds the conditions. Backtest any of them, with the biases printed above the result.

Watchlists

With your columns. Price and daily change are two of fifty available measures, not the only two.

Portfolio

Concentration, sector weights, a correlation matrix, position sizing, and look-through: your true exposure to one company across every fund you hold.

Alerts on the business

Margin compression, dilution, rising debt, dividend cuts — never on price. Every threshold is visible and editable.

Sixteen markets

The US in full, plus India, the UK, Japan, Germany, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and more. Prices, returns and dividends everywhere; filings where they exist.

Explanations, not jargon

Tap any measure for what it means. Long-press any financial term anywhere for a written, offline definition.

What it will not do

Stated in the app too, on its own screen, because an app that tells you where its edges are is easier to rely on inside them.

  • No verdicts. No buy, sell or hold. No price targets, no ratings, no confidence scores. Where two readings of the same figures exist, both are shown and the app takes no side.
  • No predictions. Every figure describes something already reported.
  • No filing summaries. A 10-K is longer than this model's context window, and a botched summary of a legal document is worse than none.
  • No chat box. A fixed list of questions the app can actually answer.
  • No community, no feed, no shared picks.

Privacy, concretely

The App Store label says Data Not Collected and that is literally true: no analytics, no crash reporting to a third party, no advertising identifier, no fingerprint, no cookies. There is no account and no server belonging to this app. What leaves your phone is a request for a public company's public filings, by ticker, with no identifier attached.

One purchase

No subscription, no upsell, no feature held back, no data harvested to pay for it. Export everything at any time, in full.
